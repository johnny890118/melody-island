## Research and transfer

- [Lusion v3, Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lusion-v3): source explicitly highlights reactive cursor and scroll animation; study purposeful response and strong framing. Its live site may evolve beyond the awarded version. Transfer one memorable interaction, not its palette, full loading journey or custom scrolling.
- [Bruno Simon Portfolio, Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bruno-simon-portfolio): game-like 3D navigation demonstrates recognizable interaction as identity. Preserve direct join/create controls here; never make visitors play a game to enter a listening room. The live portfolio has evolved since the 2019 award.
- [Hervé's Luni case study](https://herve.paris/en/our-work/luni): scenes share one world, predictable microinteractions connect chapters, mobile composition was planned from sketches. Transfer continuity between arrival and room plus independent mobile framing; do not copy pastel world or models.
- [Spotify Jam support](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/jam/): invitation, shared queue and host participation controls are explicit. Transfer the invitation/queue/control grouping into Melody Island's existing permissions, without importing subscriptions, accounts or recommendation dependencies.
- [Apple SharePlay HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/shareplay): individual settings remain accessible, and rejoining shared activity should be clear. Transfer separation of shared playback from local sound and a visible recovery path. This proposal does not add SharePlay integration.

These are research inputs, not evidence that Melody Island meets the awards' quality bar. Composition/motion recommendations below are our inference and design judgment.

## Three compositions within the same approved cosmic world

### A — Lunar Approach (recommended)

One intimate island crosses the edge of a vast, partly shaded moon. The moon is a distant celestial body, not a halo exactly fitting the island. Low-contrast crater detail recedes; a thin warm-lit edge and cool-violet shadows unify the island with the sky. Sparse stars at different depths and the phoenix passing beside the castle establish scale. No dotted orbit or decorative coordinates.

Desktop: large editorial wordmark on left, complete island silhouette across the middle/right, action group aligned with title's baseline rather than stranded in a lower corner. The scene remains directly draggable; the bird has its own flight space. Suggested focal budgets, to be tuned in render: island approximately 45–55% viewport width; moon larger and partially beyond the frame. Avoid giant blank left-above-title area.

Mobile: independently framed island occupies approximately 36–42svh in common portrait screens; tagline and full-width actions follow. Short screens scroll naturally. Never crop the island to force a no-scroll rule. Moon may crop; full island silhouette and bird resting path remain inside the safe field. One scene-animation control with a visible state label; credits stay subordinate.

Signature moment: a short, interruptible settling camera move after the real model is ready; phoenix crosses a dark/bright boundary beside it. Join/create is immediately available and never waits for the motion. Reduced motion starts at the finished camera pose.

Room expression: a composed private listening lounge. Restrained lunar arc/light field only in page margins; video, queue and typography carry the foreground. No second always-running WebGL scene.

Tradeoff: requires careful model lighting and scene-camera iteration but keeps current assets and technical stack.

### B — Eclipse

Moon sits farther behind the island with a dramatic dark side and narrow amber rim. Island is offset from the moon's center; warm castle roofs remain readable, never a black silhouette. Phoenix supplies the brightest small moving accent. Typography is more monumental and spare; entry controls have high contrast.

Room expression: theatre composition, larger video as the focal field, queue in a right panel on desktop and an accessible drawer on mobile. Add-song remains reachable; local sound activation sits outside the video. Original YouTube controls stay visible.

Tradeoff: strongest poster-like contrast, but dark architecture risks losing the product's distinctive miniature detail; opening queue adds an interaction. Evaluate contrast and mobile legibility before selecting.

### C — Island After Dark

Distant smaller moon high at the edge, much more surrounding negative space, warm windows/roofs and readable original trees against a deep plum night. The island is larger/closer than the background body. Phoenix moves slowly across the upper scene, with no invented particle trail. More intimate fantasy than astronomical spectacle.

Room expression: a music-session journal with large current-song typography, visible video and a neatly numbered shared queue. Keep current, next and add-song together; membership stays a compact secondary area.

Tradeoff: strongest cozy brand character, but targeted material/window lighting may require model-level work and can drift away from the original asset if overdone. Do not introduce lantern assets or rebuild the island unapproved.

## Recommended room layout shared by all proposals

Desktop around 1440px: room identity and honest connection/member state above; invite and members adjacent. Main grid approximately 2:1 with a visible 16:9 YouTube player left and shared queue right. Under video: current title and compact local sound state. Queue header contains 加歌, current track gets a text 播放中 marker and upcoming tracks use ordered rows. Search opens intentionally as a panel; preserve the queue behind it and return keyboard focus on close. Management remains a separate disclosure, not a third primary column.

Mobile around 390px: compact header, video, current-song/device state, then queue. 加歌 stays available near the queue and in the compact listening controls. Search opens a shadcn sheet with its own clear label, useful results and explicit no-results/loading/error states. Persistent transport carries a short current title, shared play/pause, next and local sound; less-used previous/shuffle can move into an accessible overflow. Confirm this grouping with owner, since it changes control placement. Respect bottom safe-area and virtual keyboard; no full-screen fixed-height assumption.

Arrival states:
- Room playing, device blocked: 島上正在播放 + 此裝置尚未開啟聲音 + 開始聆聽.
- Room paused: 島上已暫停; shared-play permission controls the main action, not local activation.
- Buffering: 本機正在緩衝, do not claim all participants are stalled or trigger a shared pause.
- Reconnecting: distinguish room connection loss from a YouTube-only loading issue; keep visible retry where it belongs.
- Read-only guest: show why shared transport is unavailable; local mute remains available.
- Empty queue: one factual sentence and 加入第一首歌; no fake activity or AI recommendations.

## Implementation boundaries after selection

Preserve all canonical server commands, IDs, playback versioning, clock sampling and correction controller. Interface animation never drives media time, publishes commands or consumes YouTube audio for a fabricated synchronized waveform. No extra component/icon library. Local shadcn wrappers and Lucide only.

Retain one homepage canvas, bounded DPR, hidden-tab stop and reduced-motion support. Do not add video sky textures, costly full-screen blur or mandatory postprocessing. Start with lighting, composition and optimized moon shading. Any new texture requires source/license/provenance; no unreviewed image replaces the real island.

## Approval and verification plan (not completed work)

After owner selects composition and room topology, make a narrowly scoped high-fidelity prototype at 1440×960, 1280×720, 390×844 and 390×600. Review actual model silhouette, bird direction/path, Chinese long titles and 0/1/100-item queue states. Compare normal/reduced motion. Only after owner review implement the shared layout and polish.

Acceptance targets: clear join/create without tour; one local activation gesture; immediate add-song access; readable text and visible focus; no horizontal page overflow or transport obstruction; YouTube frame unobscured; no regression in current synchronization fixtures. Performance must be measured against the same baseline/devices before claiming improvement. Aim for stable rendering and no visual-quality regression under adaptive quality; no unmeasured 60fps claim.

No main merge, production Firebase operation, new optional product feature, or full UI implementation is part of this planning stage.
